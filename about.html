Our Profile & Mission

Advancing Human Resource Development

The Indian Society for Training & Development (ISTD) Thrissur Chapter is dedicated to promoting better and more effective utilization of human resources through continuous education, training, and development. We bridge the gap between industry and academia, offering innovative programs, networking opportunities, and professional certifications to equip trainers and organizational leaders for the future.

Operating as an integral regional chapter of the national ISTD body (established in April 1970 and headquartered in New Delhi), the Thrissur Chapter serves as Central Kerala's vibrant epicenter for trainers, HR directors, behavioral coaches, institutional educators, and business consultants.

Key Initiatives

What We Do

We organize impactful programs, academic initiatives, and industry dialogues across three core strategic pillars.

01

Regional Training Programs, Workshops & Webinars

We organize regional training programs, workshops, and webinars addressing modern topics such as AI in HR, advanced instructional design, behavioral psychology, and experiential facilitation.

02

Flagship PGP-TL&D Program Facilitation

We facilitate the flagship Post Graduate Program in Training, Learning & Development (PGP-TL&D), a prestigious 15-month diploma recognized by the Govt. of India for aspiring L&D leaders.

03

Industry-Academia Collaboration

We provide interactive networking platforms for industry-academia collaboration, linking university researchers and business leaders to solve contemporary talent development challenges.

National Heritage

Affiliated with ISTD National Body, New Delhi

Established in April 1970, ISTD is a premier national non-profit professional society with 45+ chapters across India. It is an active member of the International Federation of Training and Development Organizations (IFTDO), USA and Asian Association of Training and Development Organizations (ARTDO), Manila.
