Membership

Information on ISTD membership categories, documented benefits, eligibility, fee structure, application steps, and chapter membership enquiries.

Membership CategoriesMembership BenefitsEligibilityMembership FeesHow to ApplyMembership Enquiry
01 • Categories

Membership Categories

ISTD membership is open to individuals and institutions engaged in training and human resource development. Select a category below to pre-fill the Membership Enquiry form.

Practitioners & EducatorsActive

Individual Membership

For professionals, trainers, HR managers, consultants, and faculty members involved in training and development (available as Annual or Life Membership under ISTD National norms).

Organisations & Institutes

Institutional Membership

For public and private sector organisations, corporate enterprises, universities, colleges, and training institutions committed to human resource development.

Postgraduate Students

Student Membership

For students pursuing degrees or postgraduate programmes in Management, HRM, Social Work, Psychology, or allied disciplines who wish to build a foundation in L&D.

Allied Professionals

Associate Membership

For early-career professionals and individuals interested in the field of learning, training, and human resource development as per ISTD National criteria.

02 • Benefits

Membership Benefits

Documented professional benefits available to members of the Indian Society for Training & Development.

Conferences & Workshops

Regional & National Learning Forums

Access to regional chapter sessions, workshops, seminars, and national ISTD conferences and conventions across India.

Research & Publications

Indian Journal of Training & Development (IJTD)

Subscription to ISTD’s quarterly research journal, the Indian Journal of Training & Development (IJTD), and chapter knowledge updates as per national membership terms.

Professional Contribution

Knowledge Sharing & Authorship

Opportunities to present papers, facilitate learning sessions, and contribute articles to chapter forums and ISTD publications.

Peer Community

HR & L&D Practitioner Network

Connect with corporate trainers, HR professionals, academicians, and organisational leaders in Thrissur and across ISTD chapters nationwide.

03 • Eligibility

Eligibility

Membership eligibility is governed by the constitution and rules of the Indian Society for Training & Development (ISTD), New Delhi:

  • Individual Membership: Open to individuals engaged in the training and development of human resources in government, public or private sector enterprises, educational or training institutions, and consultancy roles.
  • Institutional Membership: Open to corporate bodies, public and private sector undertakings, academic universities/colleges, and professional institutions interested in promoting training and development.
  • Student / Associate Membership: Open to students enrolled in recognised degree or diploma programmes in management, human resource development, or allied areas, and individuals meeting ISTD’s associate criteria.
04 • Fee Structure

Membership Fees

All membership entrance fees, annual/life subscription amounts, and applicable GST are set and revised by the ISTD National Office, New Delhi across Individual (Annual / Life), Institutional, Student, and Senior Citizen categories.

Because national fee schedules are administered centrally by ISTD New Delhi, please refer to the official ISTD National website for the current fee tariff or submit a Membership Enquiry below so our Thrissur Chapter Treasurer can share the latest verified national fee schedule and payment instructions.

05 • Application Process

How to Apply

Follow these steps to enquire about membership with the ISTD Thrissur Chapter or complete your formal national registration.

Step 1

Select Your Category

Review the Individual, Institutional, Student, or Associate membership categories above and choose the option matching your professional or academic profile.

Step 2

Submit a Chapter Enquiry

Use the Membership Enquiry form below or contact the Thrissur Chapter office bearers (+91 9846101034 / +91 9496839168) for guidance on affiliating with the Thrissur Chapter.

Step 3

National Portal Registration

Complete the formal application, supporting document upload, and fee payment via the official ISTD National Portal (istd.in), selecting the Thrissur (Trichur) Chapter.
