Why Campus Chapters Matter

Nurturing the Next Generation of Professionals

An ISTD Student Chapter embeds continuous learning, practitioner mentorship, and leadership development directly inside higher education campuses—empowering students before they transition into industry.

Pillar 01

Connect Academic Learning with Professional Practice

Bridge classroom theory with contemporary industry expectations, real-world case studies, organisational dynamics, and applied problem-solving.

Pillar 02

Engage Directly with Experienced Practitioners

Provide students and faculty direct access to industry leaders, corporate trainers, HR heads, and senior consultants from the national ISTD network.

Pillar 03

Develop Future-Ready Professionals & Leaders

Cultivate communication, leadership, teamwork, executive presence, and career readiness through student-led forums, workshops, and mentorship.

Pillar 04

Strengthen Academia–Industry Collaboration

Enable joint faculty development programmes (FDPs), research dialogues, internships, and campus-to-corporate developmental pathways.

Start a Campus Chapter

How to Establish an ISTD Student Chapter

Higher education institutions in Trichur and Central Kerala can partner with the ISTD Trichur Chapter to establish an official Student Chapter on their campus.

Step 1

Submit Expression of Interest (EOI)

Complete the institutional EOI form with details of your institution and nominated Faculty Coordinator.

Step 2

Consultation with Chapter Office Bearers

Our Managing Committee coordinates with your Principal/Director and Faculty Advisor on student chapter norms and annual calendar.

Step 3

Official Inauguration & Induction

Host the formal inauguration, lamp-lighting ceremony, and maiden practitioner session on your campus.
