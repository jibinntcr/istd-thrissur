Institutional Identity

National Legacy & ISTD Trichur Diamond Chapter Mandate

The Indian Society for Training & Development (ISTD), established in April 1970 in New Delhi under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, is India’s premier national autonomous society devoted to training, learning, and human resource development across industry, government, and academia.

Inaugurated on 30 March 2026 as ISTD’s Diamond Chapter under the mentorship of Prof. (Dr.) Zakkariya K. A. (Founder & Mentor, ISTD Trichur Chapter & National Vice President, ISTD) and the chairmanship of Tony Chacko, with Sneha Rose George (Secretary) and Jibin N (Treasurer), the ISTD Trichur Chapter serves as a dynamic hub for professional learning, management research, and capability building across Central Kerala.

Inauguration of First Student Chapter at Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous)

ISTD Trichur Chapter successfully inaugurated its first student chapter at Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous), creating a dedicated forum for students to connect academic learning with professional practice, engage with experienced practitioners, and develop as future professionals.

The inaugural ceremony featured the keynote address and official inauguration by Prof. (Dr.) Zakkariya K. A. (National Vice President, ISTD & Founder-Mentor, ISTD Trichur Chapter), alongside an address by Tony Chacko (Chairman, ISTD Trichur Chapter).
