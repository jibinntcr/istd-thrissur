Institutional Identity

National Legacy & Thrissur Diamond Chapter Mandate

The Indian Society for Training & Development (ISTD), established in April 1970 in New Delhi under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, is India’s premier national autonomous society devoted to training, learning, and human resource development across industry, government, and academia.

Inaugurated on 30 March 2026 as ISTD’s Diamond Chapter under the mentorship of Prof. (Dr.) Zakkariya K. A. (National Vice President, ISTD) and the chairmanship of Tony Chacko, the ISTD Thrissur Chapter serves as a collaborative forum for professional learning, management research, and capability building in Central Kerala.

ISTD Vision

“Leading Transformation through Training & Development.”

Chapter Mission

To advance training and development practices by connecting professionals, organisations, and educators through continuous learning.

First Student Chapter • ISTD Thrissur📍 Irinjalakuda, Thrissur

Inauguration of First Student Chapter at Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous)

ISTD Thrissur Chapter successfully inaugurated its first student chapter at Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous), creating a dedicated forum for students to connect academic learning with professional practice, engage with experienced practitioners, and develop as future professionals.

The inaugural ceremony featured the keynote address and official inauguration by Prof. (Dr.) Zakkariya K. A., National Vice President, ISTD, alongside an address by Tony Chacko, Chairman of the ISTD Thrissur Chapter.
