ISTD Thrissur Chapter Inauguration Photographs

Held on 30 March 2026 at Hotel Pearl Regency, Thrissur, the inauguration was attended by National President Shri. Atul Shah, Past National President Dr. Yogesh Kumar Upadhyay, National Treasurer Shri. Nabaghana Pany, Regional Vice President (South) Prof. (Dr.) Zakkariya K A, and Chapter Chairman Shri. Tony Chacko.
