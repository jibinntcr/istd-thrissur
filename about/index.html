National & Chapter Profile

Advancing Training & Human Resource Development

ISTD National Body (Established April 1970, New Delhi)

The Indian Society for Training & Development (ISTD), established in April 1970, is a national non-profit professional society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860. It has a broad membership of individuals and institutions involved in the area of training and development of human resources from government, public and private sector organisations, educational and training institutions, and other professional bodies. ISTD is affiliated to the International Federation of Training and Development Organizations (IFTDO), Geneva.

ISTD Thrissur Chapter (Inaugurated 30 March 2026)

The ISTD Thrissur Chapter (also known as the ISTD Trichur Chapter) was inaugurated on 30 March 2026 at Hotel Pearl Regency, Thrissur, marking the establishment of the Diamond Chapter of ISTD in its 55th year. The chapter brings together trainers, educators, HR professionals, academicians, and industry practitioners across the Thrissur region for continuous learning, knowledge exchange, and collaborative human resource development.

Purpose & Direction

Vision & Mission

Guided by the national mandate of ISTD and our commitment to regional professional development.

Official ISTD Vision

ISTD National Vision & Mission

Vision: “Leading Transformation through Training & Development.”

Mission: To facilitate individuals and organisations to excel in Training & Development and serve as a reservoir of universal HRD knowledge.

Chapter Objectives

Thrissur Chapter Mission & Focus

To foster a culture of continuous learning and professional development among trainers, educators, HR practitioners, and organisational leaders in the Thrissur region.

To serve as a collaborative professional body in Central Kerala connecting industry and academia through practical workshops, seminars, and peer knowledge sharing.

Chapter Activities

What We Do

Key activities organised by the ISTD Thrissur Chapter for members and the wider professional community.

01 • Learning Sessions

Training Programmes, Workshops & Webinars

Organising regional training sessions, webinars, and seminars on contemporary topics such as AI in workplace learning, instructional design, and facilitation practices.

02 • Practitioner Exchange

Professional Networking & Knowledge Sharing

Creating opportunities for HR professionals, corporate trainers, consultants, and faculty members to exchange case studies, methodologies, and practical insights.

03 • Regional Collaboration

Industry–Academia Engagement

Connecting management institutions, universities, and business organisations in Thrissur to encourage dialogue on skill development and workplace readiness.
